    Settle with RJC and DSA before Pour 1
    • The joint itself. 11/S006 draws this step as one pour, so a joint here is one not shown. It needs RJC approval (spec 1.6.3, 3.7.9), and RJC can ask for a key or extra bars (3.7.4).
    • Lap across the joint. The Z-bar leg projects about 270 mm; the lap with the vehicle-bay bars needs about 450. That means a longer leg or added dowels. Check what AGF has already fabricated (R29 was released for fab Jun 17).
    • Bar height at the joint. 4/R29 draws the legs low in the slab; slab note 5 on S201 puts bars 40 mm from the top. This sets the split line in the bulkhead.
    • Mix in the thickening. SOG 2 (25 MPa N) or SOG 1 (35 MPa C-1 with 10 L/m³ DCI).
    • Top of SOG 2. 253.070 on A151 vs 253.075 on AGF. This sets the bulkhead top.
    • Pour length. Z2 is about 49 m long, over the 40 m maximum slab pour (spec 3.7.10).
    • Where the section changes. The FW2 grade wall in braced bays 16–14 and 10–8, the walk-off recess in bay 16–15, and the east end at the door near grid 9.
