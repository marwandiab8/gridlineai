Press play to watch the elevator go in, in order, or pick a step to see it on its own. Steps 1–4 are MCL's and the electrician's prep: Schindler won't mobilize until they're done. Each step lists what the shop drawing requires and which sheet it comes from. Colours show the trade.

    Settle before Schindler mobilizes
    1. Critical items by the Christmas break. Schindler's install supervisor walked the site on Nov 18. The elevator goes into production next week and is on site right after Christmas, and Schindler won't start until all nine items are done: disconnects mounted and fed (including the non-fused disconnect in the overhead), barricades, top landing platform, a marked laydown area, rollable access, pit electrical, overhead electrical, hoist beam stamped with its max. load (or an engineer's letter), and the hoistway complete, fire sealed and vented at the top. (Alvin and Kathy, Nov 20.)
    2. Hoist beam count. The overhead detail on sheet 3/12 shows one beam on the car C/L. Item 30 on sheet 1/12 calls for two rated hoist beams per hoistway, or alternative fall-arrest anchorage that Schindler checks before it mobilizes. Confirm with Schindler which applies.
    3. Emergency power. PC-082 (issued Nov 16) changes the battery-rescue device to emergency generator power operation at the Owner's request. The reviewed drawing still shows emergency power operation "No" and battery backup "Yes". The generator and transfer switch must run at least one elevator, with power-change pending signals to the controller (sheet 1/12 items 27, 36C).
    4. Emergency communications. RFI 325 R2 (Nov 20): analog phone cable from the demarc room to the elevator control room (Rm 108) and another to the fire alarm panel; Bell activates the line at the demarc. KJA: phone line plus an internet connection with 4 h UPS backup in the control room. Spectech: two data drops in Rm 108. Priced under PC-083 (CCN-E017, CCN-T08).
    5. Suspension belts. KJA flagged the STM-PV30 belts as non-fire-rated; Life Safety to verify acceptance.
    6. Handrail height. Sheet 7/12 shows the handrails at 2′-8 11⁄16″ [831]; DSA wants 860–920 mm above the floor. Schindler says the height can't change; DSA noted it subject to OBC and accessibility requirements.
    7. Finishes and COP. PC-082 changes the walls and ceiling to the Schindler 3300 standard finish and drops the voice annunciation key switch and the emergency lighting test switch. DSA asked for a credit for the deviation from the specified finish (RFI 293).
    8. Drawing corrections. Introba asks for the local building code to read OBC instead of IBC 2020, and all previous electrical comments still apply. DSA: outstanding items to be submitted for final review and records.
    9. Front wall. Keep the rough openings in the CMU front wall open until the entrances are set. The front wall at the top landing waits until the temporary platform comes out.
