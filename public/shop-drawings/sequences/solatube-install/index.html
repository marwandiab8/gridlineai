    Settle with DSA, RJC and Solatube before the mock-up
    • Cap fit over the roofing. The 35″ metal curb inside the 35⅝″ cap leaves about 8 mm a side. Two-ply SBS (≈7 mm) plus a term bar (≈3 mm) likely won’t fit under the skirt, and Solatube’s cut sheet says its curbs are designed for single-ply. Dry-fit a cap on the mock-up curb.
    • VB and air barrier at the curb. Confirm the tie-in at the curb and the rough opening (spec 08 63 00 3.2.5, RJC’s comment on Triumph’s submittal).
    • Thermal Insulation Panel or trim ring ‘R’. RFI 251: DSA asked for an interface detail. A TIP sits in the insulation layer and changes the tube lengths.
    • Reviewed R1. R1 has to show the ETL 40″ tube (R0 showed 20″), with the P.Eng design and the engineered thermal and air/vapour package.
    • Dome. The shop drawing says DP; the spec and the cut sheet say DPP.
    • Cap screws. Solatube’s instructions say 6 per side; the DSA-requested curb markup shows 4″ o.c.
    • Deck opening. Reinforcement around the Ø749 opening per structural.
    • Cold weather. Seals and tape need 70 °F (21 °C) or warmer. A November install needs heat and hoarding or Solatube’s written cold-weather procedure.
    • Locations and clearances. The 6 units aren’t tagged on A104 Rev.24. The diffuser hangs about 1.05 m below the deck; keep 6″ (152 mm) clear of joists, sprinklers, the EV cable tray and fans.
