    Settle before the slab goes in
    • Oil/sand interceptor. DSA and IRB accepted MCL's new location just outside grid A (RFI 326 R1, Nov 4). Revised mechanical, civil and structural shop drawings are still due. The slab opening on A151 and the old routing no longer apply. SK-01 / RFI 315 set the pit for 3100 deep at the old spot; confirm the depth and detail for the new one.
    • Water table. SK-01 shows the water table at 252.100. The pit base is near 249.75, over 2 m below it. Plan dewatering, and shore any trench deeper than 1.2 m (RJC note).
    • D3 area drain. F2C found the D3 drain pipe 1450 west of design (4561 east of grid 8). Move the pipe, or move the drain and re-grade its falls (A151 note 10: 2–5%).
    • Electrical under the slab. The conduit runs in this sketch are placeholders. Get the real runs from O'Connor Electric and the electrical drawings before the granular goes down.
    • Trench lengths and outlets. R49 asks to coordinate trench drain lengths with MSB. The Watts channels are pre-sloped with 4″ bottom outlets. Fix run lengths and outlet positions before the mud slabs.
    • Insulation and vapour barrier at the trenches. This sketch leaves them out of the trench thickenings, as agreed at grid H. Confirm with RJC/DSA, and confirm how the vapour barrier laps into the troughs.
    • SOG 5 strip. R49 shows SOG 5 (200, no topping) on the east side of the Phase-2 trench near grid 7. This sketch draws SOG 1 + topping everywhere. Confirm the extent on S201.
    • Mud slab. Thickness and mix aren't specified. The sketch assumes about 75 of lean concrete. Confirm with RJC.
    • Radiant shop drawings. The revised Kolostat submittal (23 83 00-2.1) came back Revise and Resubmit on Nov 5. Settle it before the tubing goes down.
    • Pour plan. The bay is about 72 × 38 m. Spec 03 30 00 caps a pour at 40 m (cl. 3.7.10), so plan the pour breaks. Topping joints must sit over the slab joints (cl. 3.10.3). The joint at grid H is covered in the Grid H bulkhead sequence.
