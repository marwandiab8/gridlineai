    What this model assumes
    • The deck shape is the 221 F2C readings (surveyed Nov 5, 2026, revised Nov 11), joined by straight lines. The main roof and the north strip meet at the step on grid H.
    • The roof is treated as a smooth surface at top-of-deck shape, as if the roofing went down at one thickness. The Accu-Plane R5 tapered insulation is not in here. Use Build up to try crickets and fill.
    • Roof edges, the stair, the elevator overrun and the deck openings act as solid curbs. Water leaves only through drains.
    • Each drain location takes water at its lower surveyed reading, flush with the deck, up to the drain size you set. That size is an assumption, not the plumbing design.
    • Most drains sit about half a metre off their valley line, so on the bare deck a shallow stream can run past them. The sump setting dishes the roof at every drain over a 1.2 m radius to show what a sump changes.
    • Flow is a shallow-water calculation on a 0.25 m grid with the roughness of a smooth membrane (Manning 0.013). Use it for where water goes and where it sits, not for depths to the millimetre.
    • This is a planning model for coordination. It is not a substitute for the roofing designer's taper layout or the engineer's design.
