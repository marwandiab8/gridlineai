    Settle before the cooler is delivered
    • Floor depression. The 5″ depression (9′-1″ × 11′-1½″, level to ¼″ in 10′) has to be in the slab. MCL's note on the submittal: DSA to issue an SI for it.
    • Service space above the unit. Bohn asks for 2′ clear above the PT0078 to lift its top panel. The unit is about 20″ tall and the section shows 24″ (to be confirmed) from the cooler to the structure, which leaves about 4″.
    • Ventilation of the space above. With the top enclosure panels fixed, that space has to be ventilated by others so the unit sees 90 °F (32 °C) or less. It rejects 11,600 BTU/h. Confirm with mechanical who provides it and how.
    • Power. The unit comes with a cord and needs a NEMA 6-15R receptacle (208–230 V, MCA 9.7 A, MOPD 15 A). IM4, the light and the heaters need 120 V/15 A, and the dry contact goes to the alarm system. Per Norbec there's only one unit and one light fixture.
    • Floor finish. The inside and outside floor finish blanks on page 2/11 are empty. The 5″ depression includes the floor finish, so confirm what goes on it.
    • Bumpers and handle. The drawing shows grey recycled plastic bumpers; DSA wants HDPE white. The handle is chrome per DSA.
    • Grout in front of the door. Noted "by others" in detail A. Assign it.
    • Seismic screws. The moldings fix to the building wall with concrete screws #14 × 1½″ @ 16″ for block, Tek screws for steel studs. Confirm the wall type at the north, east and south walls.
    • Site verification. The sub verifies all dimensions on site and coordinates delivery with the MCL superintendent (MCL comment).
