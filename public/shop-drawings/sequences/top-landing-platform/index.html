Press play to watch the platform go in, in order, or pick a step to see it on its own. Schindler only gives a spec on sheet 3/12; the design, the build, the upkeep and the removal are MCL's. Each step lists what the drawing or the regulation requires. Brown is MCL, blue is Schindler, teal is an O. Reg. 213/91 requirement, red is a hold.

    Settle before you build
    1. Design. Sheet 3/12 says the platform is "designed & provided by others" and note 35 on 1/12 puts erection, maintenance and removal on others too, securely fastened to the structure and to code. Nothing engineered is on file, and Schindler drawing TD440 (referenced in note 35) isn't in Drive. Ask Schindler for TD440 and get an engineer's letter covering the capacity and the angle anchors.
    2. Rated load. Schindler asks for 1,000 lb [454 kg] (note 2). O. Reg. 213/91 s. 134 requires every work platform to be designed for at least 2.4 kN/m²: about 8.8 kN [2,000 lb] spread over this 5′-11⅞″ × 6′-7⅜″ (3.7 m²) deck. The engineer sets the number you post.
    3. Front wall anchors. The front angle goes into the CMU front wall just under the Level 2 slab edge. Grouted cells or through-bolts, as the engineer specifies.
    4. Fall protection anchorage. Sheet 3/12 shows one hoist beam on the car C/L. Item 30 on 1/12 asks for two rated hoist beams per hoistway, or alternative anchorage (5,000 lb per person attached, or an engineered system) that Schindler checks before it mobilizes. Confirm which applies.
    5. Deck level. Note 5 wants the deck level with the top floor opening. If the Level 2 topping isn't poured yet when the platform goes in, agree the deck height with Schindler's superintendent.
    6. Crew and date. No crew or sub is named for it in Drive yet. It's critical item 3: Schindler won't mobilize until it's in, and the elevator is on site right after Christmas (Alvin and Kathy, Nov 20). Removal is on Schindler's call (note 1), and the front wall at the top landing waits until it's out (note 6).
