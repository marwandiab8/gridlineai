    Settle before the car wash goes in
    • Room size. The shop drawing shows the room 39′-7″ × 34′-11″ inside. A105 and M202 scale a little larger. Lay out the rails from the real walls: start 1′-8″ off the north wall, centreline 10′-8″ off the west wall, centred on the TD-1 / CB-1 trench.
    • Wall sleeve. Istobal wants an 8″ × 12″ sleeve at 8′-6″ AFF, by the GC (sheet 3/7). Its location isn't shown and MCL asked. With the equipment inside the bay it may not be needed. Confirm with National Wash.
    • Air compressor. Istobal needs one (2 HP, 26 gal, 145 psi min.) and it isn't on the mechanical drawings. RFI 080 (Sep 2025) says it will be added on the next mechanical change notice. Confirm it was, and where it goes.
    • Dryers. The gantry load table (4/7) includes the lateral and contouring dryers, 67 A of the 88.4 A FLA. The scope review excluded the dryer (optional price). Settle it before the gantry circuit is sized.
    • Seismic and supports. Spec 2.2.6 calls for P.Eng-sealed seismic design (OBC 4.1.8.18, CSA S832), and M202 notes the building is post-disaster. The scope review excludes seismic and supports for mounting equipment. Decide who designs and supplies them.
    • Covers. The pump, chemical and control gear sits inside the wet bay. Istobal note 9 says some of it needs special covers. Confirm they're in National Wash's price.
    • Brushes and options. Brush colour is DSA's pick (MCL asked for the catalogue on 2/7). The scope review had MCL confirming the wheel wash, vinyl wrap, on-board signs and dryer by RFI. Check the answers made it into the order.
    • Clearances. Istobal needs a 10′-0″ min. door and 12′-6″ min. clear inside. S506 scales the openings about 12′ high and the underside of the Level 2 deck at 4.75 m (15′-7″). Keep lights, sprinklers and ducts above 12′-6″ over the lane.
    • Reclaim. No water recirculation (the spec doesn't call for it), so note 5 (reclaim piping PVC Sch. 80) doesn't apply.
    • Revision. The file is named REV 5.0 but the title blocks read rev 4.0 (May 7 2025). It went to DSA, M&E and structural for approval on Aug 20 2025. Confirm the reviewed copy.
    • Wash-Me Express. The vehicle cleaning station (spec 2.3) is located by SI-032 (RFI 94). It isn't in this animation.
