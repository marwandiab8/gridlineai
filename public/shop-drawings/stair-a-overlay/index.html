Stair A west wall: EG10B stops 255 mm short of the pier

Plan of the stair's west wall with north at the top and south at the bottom, like A105. The outside (west) is on the left, the stair is on the right. It runs from the stair's northwest corner (top) to the start of EG10C (bottom).

  • Press Architect vs curtain wall to see only blue and red. Across the wall they match: same glass line, same corner.
  • Along the wall they don't. The blue jamb at the framed pier sits about 255 mm further south than the red one. That's glass Bothwell left out.
  • Why: grid J falls inside this opening, so A105 dimensions it in two pieces, 225 + 2775. Bothwell took 2775 as the whole opening and started it at the corner.
  • EG10C starts in the same place on both, so the pier between them grows from 300 to about 555 mm.

Entrance vestibule corner: S201B walls don't match the curb or the glass

Plan of the vestibule's northwest corner at grids I and 16, north up. EG12 with the sliding door D103 runs east along the top. EG13 runs south down the left.

  • The red line is the glass. The green dashed L is the curb on A151 and on the curtain wall drawings, just inside the glass. The purple L is where S201B draws the concrete.
  • North wall: S201B is 32 mm further north. Still behind the glass, but 22 mm behind it instead of 53.
  • West wall: S201B shows it 250 thick and pushed west, so 60 mm of concrete sits outside the glass (red hatch).
  • RFI 201 changed these curbs to 250 mm, but no revised sketch says which face stays put.
