What GridlineAI offers

The field reporting workflow your team will actually use.

No complicated mobile app rollout. Crews send normal texts, photos, and voice notes. Management gets structured records, reports, and visibility in the dashboard.

SMS

Text-based daily logs

Capture progress, safety, delays, deliveries, inspections, deficiencies, and punch items directly from the field.

MMS

Photo evidence, linked automatically

Site photos are saved and connected to the right message or report context, so visual proof does not get lost in camera rolls.

PDF

Daily reports on demand
